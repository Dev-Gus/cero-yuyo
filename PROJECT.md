# Cero Yuyo: website

## Goal
One-page website for Cero Yuyo, a lawn care service in Tacuarembó, Uruguay, run by Gustavo González. The site is also a real proof project for his web studio, Tacuara Lab. It must be honest about that.

## Decisions
- Name: Cero Yuyo (exact spelling, two words).
- Spanish only, single page, plain direct Spanish (voseo), no jargon.
- Services offered today: Corte de pasto; Bordeado; Limpieza de terrenos; Poda de arbustos y árboles chicos; Canteros y plantaciones.
- Pruning is offered for small shrubs and trees. The owner has no ladder yet, so no tall work.
- No price list. The hook is: "Mandame una foto del terreno" and get a free quote.
- Contact: WhatsApp only. Number: wa.me/59898871858 with a prefilled message. No form, no email.
- Honest label: the site states it is Gustavo's own business, built by Tacuara Lab. No fake testimonials, no fake or stock photos of jobs, no "coming soon".
- Real owner-supplied photos are live: `hero.jpg` and original job pairs 1, 3, 6 and 8, in that order. Processed assets are `hero.webp` and `job1` through `job4` with `-before.webp`/`-after.webp` suffixes; processed job2 comes from trabajo3, job3 from trabajo6 and job4 from trabajo8.
- Service area: only the city of Tacuarembó.
- The typed `jobs` array contains four before/after pairs with neutral alt text and empty optional descriptions. `Trabajos recientes`, its exact supplied intro and the footer's `Trabajos` link render only when at least one complete pair exists, between Cómo trabajo and Quién soy. New pairs use the existing markup and layout.
- Optional typed `heroImage` uses imported Astro image metadata for intrinsic dimensions. Astro's built-in Image serves responsive WebP sizes; hero is eager/high priority, job photos lazy. Hero uses two columns on desktop and text then photo on mobile; pairs sit side by side from tablet up and stack on mobile.
- Photo privacy: `photos-originals/` stays ignored and is never modified, deleted, committed or served. Only nine metadata-free processed copies are published. The owner selected the photos; visual review found outdoor work, only the owner's face in the hero and no readable house numbers or plates in the published selection. Both images of trabajo6 and trabajo8 were opened and reviewed before processing: no people/faces, mail/documents, readable house numbers, plates or street names were observed. Trabajo6 has vehicles and pictogram-only traffic signs without readable identifying information. No new pair was skipped, blurred or cropped. Original job pair 2 remains excluded for the readable house number in `trabajo2-despues.jpg`; the owner's final decision revoked the temporary exception. Pairs 4, 5 and 7 remain unselected and unprocessed. `trabajoterminado.webp`, `trabajoterminado2.webp` and `trabajoterminado3.webp` are excluded because they have no matching pair.
- Manual photo optimization: `node scripts/optimize-photos.mjs` reads only the nine selected originals and regenerates the committed WebP copies, applying EXIF orientation before stripping metadata. Quality 78, effort 6, smart subsampling; hero max width 1600px, pairs max width 1200px without enlargement (new pairs retain 1080x1080). Pair dimensions/aspect ratios must match; no crop or stretch. `--verify-dist` checks every built image for EXIF/GPS, ICC, XMP and IPTC. The script uses sharp already installed by Astro and is not part of the build; README.md documents both commands. The existing 262-byte SVG favicon is retained.
- Warm cream, deep green and decorative lime palette documented in DESIGN.md; tokens live in src/styles/global.css.
- Temporary `noindex` is active until the accountant answers whether registration is required before advertising. Remove it before promoting the site.
- Canonical is omitted because config has no complete workers.dev URL. No structured data, social preview image or analytics in this task.
- Fast (Lighthouse 90+), mobile first.
- Hosted on Cloudflare Workers static assets, free workers.dev URL for now. No custom domain until the owner decides.
